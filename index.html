Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Solar

Got questions before making the switch? Here are transparent answers to our most common inquiries.

Most residential and commercial clients eliminate 75% to 95% of their monthly electric bills. With net metering, excess electricity produced during sunny daylight hours is pushed back onto the local grid, generating credits that cancel out your night-time grid usage.
Solar photovoltaic (PV) panels generate power from ambient ultraviolet and diffused sunlight, not just direct heat. On cloudy or overcast days, panels typically generate 20% to 40% of their peak capacity. At night, your property smoothly draws from either your stored battery energy or the net-metered electric grid.
Physical installation for residential properties typically takes just 1 to 2 days! We use certified architectural watertight flashings and structural clamping systems that protect your roof shingles or tiles, backed by our 10-year roof penetration warranty.
You receive a 25-Year Linear Power Output Warranty guaranteeing the panels generate at least 85% of rated capacity at year 25, a 25-Year Inverter Manufacturer Warranty, and our Ayushi Akshay Urja Shop 10-Year Workmanship & Comprehensive Roof Protection Warranty.
A battery is optional for grid-tied systems that use net metering. However, if your area experiences frequent storm outages, grid brownouts, or your utility has aggressive Time-of-Use peak rates, adding a smart lithium battery provides complete peace of mind and keeps your refrigerators, medical gear, and essentials running seamlessly.
